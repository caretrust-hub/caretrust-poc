    Prototype engineering measures

    What the workload counters mean

    Fields prefilled
    Cited fields proposed from the synthetic referral instead of typed from scratch.
    Exceptions to review
    Low-confidence or incomplete fields deliberately routed to a person.
    App entries generated
    Purpose-limited field mappings emitted to test apps that would otherwise require separate entry.
    Human approvals left
    Coordinator review, patient sharing approval, and supervisor assignment. AI cannot remove these gates.

    These are measured prototype interactions, not validated staff time savings or field outcomes. Phase 2 testing will compare time-on-task, corrections, follow-up contacts, and onboarding completion with provider organizations and direct-care workers.

What this proves

CareTrust is the trust layer—not the caregiver app.

This small reference client represents one app a direct-care worker might already use. It receives only an approved shift projection and a decision receipt. Scheduling, billing, documents, family details, and clinical records stay elsewhere.

  1. The app requests a bounded capability for one worker, patient, purpose, and audience.
  2. CareTrust authenticates the worker through an external OIDC provider and links that identity to a participant. This demo uses a synthetic local identity.
  3. Deterministic policy checks patient approval, qualification, assignment, app registration, purpose, validity, and revocation.
  4. The app receives a one-time authorization result, short-lived audience-bound token, minimum-data projection, and receipt.

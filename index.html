Phase 1 design prototype

Caregiver workforce infrastructure · ACL Track 2

Activate a trusted care workforce across applications.

CareTrust gives provider organizations one AI-assisted workflow to compile caregiver intent, workforce credentials, assignments, documents, and app requirements into reviewable trust packages. People approve; deterministic policy gives each worker and independent app different, revocable access.

Product horizon

A focused proof with a platform path

  1. Now · executable local Provider workforce activation Multi-client queue, one-case drill-down, three caregiver contexts, app onboarding, reviewed coordination items, exact decisions, and revocation.
  2. Secondary technical proof · retained AWS Credential evidence compiler OCR and model output remain untrusted until accountable human and source gates are satisfied.
  3. Network path · local federation laboratory Independent organizations and applications Open contracts and signed metadata support a federated path without a universal caregiver or clinical-data silo; operational federation and live HIE/EHR access remain planned.

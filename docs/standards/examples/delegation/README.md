# Synthetic patient-delegation contract examples

These files form one referentially linked, wholly synthetic example chain:

1. a patient intent statement with exact character spans;
2. an AI-proposed draft that remains non-activating and non-authorizing;
3. one bounded clarification request and patient response;
4. a hashed, expiring, single-use invite and synthetic-account acceptance;
5. explicit patient approval bound to the final draft and clarification;
6. separate relationship and least-privilege delegation artifacts;
7. one application request and policy decision; and
8. a patient-directed revocation record.

The examples establish neither identity nor legal authority. Invite acceptance
does not prove relationship or consent. A relationship claim does not grant
application permission. Patient approval does not bypass an application's
independent policy. The vocabulary is locally defined for prototype testing in
[`../../caretrust-delegation-vocabulary.v1.json`](../../caretrust-delegation-vocabulary.v1.json);
it is not a FHIR profile, OAuth authorization server, or legal standard.
